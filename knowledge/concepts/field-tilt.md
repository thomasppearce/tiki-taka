# Field tilt

Field tilt is the share of play in the final third that belongs to one team. Tiki-Taka measures it as a team's share of all passes played into the attacking third: if Northgate play 30 such passes and Westmere 10, Northgate's field tilt is 75%.

## Why it matters

Possession counts every pass, including safe ones in a team's own half. Field tilt only counts the passes that put the opponent under pressure, so it tells you which side is pinning the other back. A team can have less of the ball and still dominate territory, which is common for direct and pressing teams.

## Reading it on air

- Above 65% over ten minutes: one side is camped in the other's half.
- Around 50%: the game is being played between the boxes, or both sides are trading attacks.
- A sudden swing often comes before chances, so it is a useful early signal of momentum.
