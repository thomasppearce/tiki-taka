# Expected goals (xG)

Expected goals measures the quality of a chance: the probability that a shot from that position becomes a goal, before considering how well it was struck. A shot worth 0.30 xG is scored about three times in ten.

## What drives it

Distance and angle matter most. A shot from close range in front of goal is worth far more than one from 25 metres or from a tight angle near the byline. Match Desk's model uses distance and angle only, so it describes the chance, not the finish.

## Reading it on air

- A single chance above 0.30 is a big chance.
- A team's total xG says how many goals its chances would usually produce. Leading the xG count while trailing on the scoreboard usually means wasteful finishing or a strong goalkeeper.
- Low xG goals (under 0.10) are long-range strikes or awkward angles: rare, and worth highlighting.
