# Momentum

Momentum describes which team is on top at a given point in the match. Tiki-Taka measures it from expected threat: each team's share of the threat created in recent minutes, smoothed so that one action doesn't swing it, on a scale from fully home (+1) to fully away (-1).

## Momentum shifts

A momentum shift is when the line swings clearly to one side and stays there for a few minutes. It often follows a goal, a substitution, a tactical change, or a spell of pressing, and it often comes before chances.

## Reading it on air

Momentum is about danger, not possession. A team can have the ball and no momentum if its passing goes sideways; a counter-attacking side can have momentum with very little of the ball.
