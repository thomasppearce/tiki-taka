# The low block and the counter-attack

A low block is a deep, narrow defensive shape: two lines close together near the team's own box, conceding possession and territory to deny space in behind and between the lines.

## Counter-attacking

Teams that sit in a low block aim to break quickly when they win the ball, with forwards who run into the space the opponent leaves behind. The danger is in the transition, not in long spells of possession.

## Reading it on air

- A low-block side can have well under 40% possession and still be comfortable.
- Its chances tend to be few but direct: fast breaks, through balls, and runs in behind.
- If it concedes first, it has to come out of the block, which changes the shape of the game.
