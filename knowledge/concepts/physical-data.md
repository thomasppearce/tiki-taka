# Physical data: sprints, speed, and distance

Tracking data records every player's position many times a second, which gives each player's speed, distance covered, and sprints.

## Definitions used by Match Desk

- A sprint is running faster than 25.2 km/h for at least one second.
- Top speed is the fastest a player reached during a sprint; elite players reach 33 to 36 km/h.
- Distance covered is the total ground a player has run. A typical outfield player covers 10 to 12 km in a match; midfielders usually cover the most.

## Reading it on air

Top speeds matter most for forwards running in behind and defenders recovering. A high sprint count late in a match shows a player still pressing or chasing; falling numbers can show fatigue before a substitution.
