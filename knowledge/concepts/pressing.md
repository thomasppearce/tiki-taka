# Pressing

Pressing means closing down the player on the ball to force a mistake or a hurried pass. A pressing team decides how high up the pitch to start (its pressing height) and how often to commit players to it (its intensity).

## High press

A high-pressing side engages near the opponent's goal. When it works, the ball is won close to goal and chances follow immediately. The cost is space behind the press and a high defensive line, and pressing is physically expensive, so intensity often drops late in a match.

## Counter-press

Immediately after losing the ball, the nearest players press to win it straight back before the opponent can organise. Many turnovers are reversed within a few seconds this way.

## Reading it on air

Balls won high up the pitch, many pressures, and short opponent possessions are the signs of a press that is working. Long balls over the top are the usual answer to it.
