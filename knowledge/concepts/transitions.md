# Transitions

A transition is the moment possession changes hands. Teams are at their most disorganised then: the side that has just won the ball has space ahead of it, and the side that has just lost it is out of its defensive shape.

## Attacking transitions

Breaking quickly before the opponent recovers is the main weapon of counter-attacking teams. Players who were behind the ball sprint forward; the ball is moved forward early.

## Defensive transitions

The team that loses the ball either counter-presses to win it back at once, or recovers: players caught upfield sprint back into shape. Recovery runs are a large part of a player's high-intensity running.

## Reading it on air

Goals that come within seconds of a turnover are transition goals. A team that keeps conceding them is losing the ball in dangerous areas or recovering too slowly.
