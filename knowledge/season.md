# The Meridian League: the season so far

## Overview

The Meridian League is a fictional league of 20 clubs, each playing 38 games.
After 37 rounds, everything that matters is still to be decided on the final day, when all ten
matches kick off at the same time. The champions finish first, the continental cup places are
2nd to 4th, and the bottom
three (18th to 20th) are relegated. Clubs level on points are separated
by goal difference, then goals scored.

## The title race

Northgate Rovers (1st, 82 points, goal difference +40) lead Halvern Town (2nd, 80 points, goal difference +41) by 2 points.
Halvern's goal difference is 1 better, so if
Northgate fail to win and Halvern win, Halvern are champions. Northgate host Westmere Athletic;
Halvern travel to Brackenford FC.

## The relegation battle

Dunhallow United and Lanterne Port FC are already relegated. The last relegation place
is between Westmere Athletic (17th, 37 points, goal difference -21) and Corrin Bay (18th, 35 points, goal difference -19). Westmere are 2 points
ahead, but Corrin Bay's goal difference is 2 better,
so a Corrin Bay win and a Westmere draw would send Westmere down. Westmere play the leaders,
Northgate Rovers; Corrin Bay host Millbrook Town.

## The race for fourth

Tidewick Rovers (4th, 66 points, goal difference +16), Kingsreach (5th, 65 points, goal difference +13), and Porthallow Athletic (6th, 64 points, goal difference +13) are chasing the last continental cup place.
Gullhaven FC (3rd, 71 points, goal difference +26) have third secured.
