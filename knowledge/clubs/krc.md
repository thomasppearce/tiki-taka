# Kingsreach (KRC)

A fictional club in the Meridian League. Formation: 4-4-2. Style: low block and counter.

## How they play

They defend deep in a narrow, compact block, invite the opposition forward, and break quickly when they win the ball, looking for forwards running in behind. They give up possession and territory on purpose; their danger is the speed of the transition.

## What to watch for

A deep line leaves little space in behind, so opponents have to break them down in front of the box; shots from range and crosses become the main threats. Their forwards attack the space behind the defence constantly; expect sprints, through balls, and the occasional offside flag.
